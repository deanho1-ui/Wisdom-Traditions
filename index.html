Five Wisdom Traditions

The common principles of the major religions, and the most-quoted passages of the Bible, Tao Te Ching, Quran, Buddhist sutras and the Analects of Confucius: what each one means, where it comes from, and how to live it today.

Translations: Bible from the King James Version; Tao Te Ching, Analects and Dhammapada adapted from James Legge and Max Müller (public domain); Quran and other sutras rendered in plain modern English after public-domain translations. Translations are approximate. Use the "Read the full text" links to check each passage in context. The Primer is adapted from the discussion series Common Principles of the Major Religions (2017–2018).

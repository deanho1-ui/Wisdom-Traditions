The Primer is adapted from the discussion series Common Principles of the Major Religions (Cincinnati, 2017–2018).

Translations: the Bible from the King James Version; the Tao Te Ching, Analects and Dhammapada adapted from James Legge and Max Müller (public domain); the Quran and other sutras rendered in plain modern English after public-domain translations. Translations are approximate. Use the source links to read each passage in context.
